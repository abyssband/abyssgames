About

A small, independent studio

AbyssGames is the work of Ting Feng Chou (周庭峰) — an independent developer who believes software should respect the people who use it. That means privacy by design: no accounts to create, no data sent to servers, no analytics, no tracking. The apps are self-contained and run on your hardware.

It’s a non-commercial effort. The goal is to make careful, honest tools — not to collect data or sell anything about you.
